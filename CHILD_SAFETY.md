## 1. Zero-Tolerance Policy

**12Rashi maintains an uncompromising, zero-tolerance policy towards Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Abuse and Exploitation (CSAE).**

Any depiction, facilitation, solicitation, promotion, possession, or distribution of CSAM, or any conduct that endangers, exploits, or abuses minors, is strictly prohibited on the 12Rashi platform. Violations will result in immediate permanent account termination, device bans, and immediate escalation to law enforcement authorities and international safety organizations.

- **Adult-Only Service:** 12Rashi is exclusively designed and operated as a mature dating and companion platform for adults aged **40 years and older**. The absolute minimum legal age to use the service is **18 years of age**.

## 3. Prohibited Content & Behaviors

The following activities and content are strictly prohibited and will trigger immediate enforcement:

1. **Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM):** Generating, transmitting, uploading, storing, sharing, or linking to any visual, audio, or textual depictions of child sexual abuse or exploitation.
2. **Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE):** Grooming, coercion, sextortion, child trafficking, or any attempt to solicit sexual communications or media involving or directed at minors.
3. **Depiction of Minors:** Uploading profile pictures, gallery media, or chat attachments that feature children or minors in any suggestive or sexualized manner.
4. **Facilitation or Encouragement:** Advocating for, normalizing, or providing instructions related to CSAE or CSAM.

## 5. Law Enforcement Cooperation & Mandatory Reporting

12Rashi complies with all applicable local and international child protection laws, including:

1. **National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC):**
   - In accordance with global best practices, any confirmed or suspected instance of CSAM/CSAE is reported directly to NCMEC's **CyberTipline** ([report.cybertip.org](https://report.cybertip.org) / 1-800-843-5678).

2. **National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (India):**
   - Suspected incidents within India are escalated immediately to the **Ministry of Home Affairs Cyber Crime Portal** ([cybercrime.gov.in](https://cybercrime.gov.in) / Helpline 1930) and local law enforcement under the **Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012** and the **Information Technology Act, 2000**.

3. **Evidence Preservation:**
   - In cases of CSAM/CSAE, 12Rashi securely preserves account metadata, IP logs, timestamps, and related digital evidence to assist law enforcement agencies in prosecution.
