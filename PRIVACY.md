## 3. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide to Us
- **Account Registration:** Full name, phone number, email address, date of birth, gender, marital status, and preferred language.
- **Profile Details:** Dating preferences, astrological details (Rashi / Zodiac sign, Nakshatra), interests, occupation, education, bio, and lifestyle habits.
- **Photos & Media:** Profile pictures and gallery photos uploaded by you to showcase your dating profile.
- **Messages & Interactions:** In-app messages, likes, matches, bookmarks, and chat histories with other users.
- **Identity & Age Verification (Optional):** Government ID or selfie verification to obtain a "Verified" trust badge, processed via encrypted channels.

### B. Information Automatically Collected
- **Device & Technical Data:** Device model, operating system version, unique device identifiers, IP address, and network information.
- **Approximate Location:** City/region information derived from IP or user-selected location to present geographically relevant dating matches. We do **not** continuously track fine-grained background GPS without user consent.
- **Usage & Activity Logs:** Time and duration of app usage, features accessed, and interaction logs.

We do **NOT** sell, rent, or trade your personal information to data brokers or third-party advertisers.

- **Public Profile Visibility:** Information you choose to make public (first name, age, zodiac sign, photos, bio, interests, city) is visible to other verified members of 12Rashi. Sensitive contact details (phone number, email, private government IDs) are never made public.
- **Trusted Service Providers:** We work with reputable cloud infrastructure and verification partners (e.g., Google Cloud Platform) bound by strict confidentiality and data protection agreements.
- **Legal & Safety Requirements:** We may disclose information if required by court order, subpoena, or applicable law, or to protect the safety of our users, public, or platform integrity.

## 6. Data Security

We implement rigorous technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data:
- **Encryption:** All data in transit is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.2 / TLS 1.3). Sensitive credentials and profile records are encrypted at rest.
- **Access Controls:** Restricted employee access to production databases governed by role-based access protocols.
- **Continuous Monitoring:** Regular automated vulnerability scans and security audits.

## 7. Data Retention & Account Deletion

### Retention Policy
We retain your personal data only as long as necessary to provide our matchmaking services or until you delete your account.

### How to Delete Your Account and Data
You maintain full control over your personal data. You can delete your account and associated data at any time:
1. **In-App:** Open **12Rashi** > Tap **Profile / Settings** > Scroll to **Account Security** > Tap **Delete Account**.
2. **Via Email Request:** Send an email from your registered email address to **getvasu2003@gmail.com** with the subject line *"Account Deletion Request"*.

Upon receiving an account deletion request:
- Your profile, photographs, and mutual match listings are immediately removed from discovery.
- All stored personal data is permanently purged from active systems within **30 days**, except for records required by statutory law or fraud dispute resolution.
