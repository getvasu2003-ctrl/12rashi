Child Safety Standards (CSAE & CSAM Policy)

App Name: 12Rashi Mature Dating App
Last Updated: October 12, 2026

Zero Tolerance Commitment: 12Rashi maintains an uncompromising, zero-tolerance policy regarding Child Sexual Abuse Material (CSAM) and Child Sexual Exploitation and Abuse (CSAE). We strictly prohibit any activities, media, or communications that harm, exploit, or endanger children.

1. Strict Age Requirement (40+ / Adult Only)

12Rashi is exclusively designed and operated as a mature dating platform tailored for individuals aged 40 and older. The minimum legal age permitted to access or use the application under any circumstance is 18 years of age.

  • Minors (individuals under the age of 18) are strictly prohibited from creating accounts, accessing, or interacting on the platform.
  • Accounts found to belong to or represent minors are terminated immediately.

2. Prohibited Behaviors & Content

The following actions will result in immediate permanent account termination, device bans, and immediate referral to criminal law enforcement:

  • CSAM: Uploading, sharing, generating, soliciting, or storing Child Sexual Abuse Material.
  • CSAE: Child grooming, coercive communications, sextortion, child trafficking, or any predatory behavior directed at or involving minors.
  • Underage Content: Sharing or displaying images of children in sexualized or abusive contexts.

3. Reporting Mechanisms

A. In-App Reporting

Users can report any profile, chat message, or image directly within the app using the "Report Profile" button located on every user profile and chat window. Reports categorized under Child Safety receive highest-priority immediate review.

4. Law Enforcement Escalation

In accordance with global and local legal obligations, 12Rashi actively coordinates with statutory authorities:

  • NCMEC: Any confirmed instance or suspicion of CSAM is reported directly to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline (report.cybertip.org).
  • India Cyber Crime Portal: Escalated to the Indian Ministry of Home Affairs Cyber Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in / Helpline 1930) and local enforcement under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012.
  • Digital Evidence: User credentials, IP logs, and evidence records are preserved securely to aid law enforcement investigations.

5. Ongoing Prevention & Moderation

We implement proactive detection, keyword blacklists, automated media screening, and manual moderation to prevent abusive content from ever reaching platform members.
