We do not sell your personal information. We only share information with:

4. Data Security

We enforce industry-standard security safeguards, including TLS 1.3 encryption in transit, encryption at rest, secure session tokens, and strict access controls to safeguard your data.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

You have full ownership of your data. You may request account deletion at any time directly through the app settings or by contacting our support team. Upon deletion, your profile and media are permanently removed from active matchmaking systems within 30 days.

6. Age Requirement

12Rashi is strictly reserved for users aged 40 and older (minimum legal age: 18+). Individuals under the age of 18 are prohibited from using the service.
